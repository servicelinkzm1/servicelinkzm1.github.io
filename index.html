Privacy and terms

How ServiceLink works with you.

Last updated 7 November 2026. This page explains what information ServiceLink keeps, why, and the rules for using the platform.

1. Who we are

ServiceLink is run by ServiceLink Business Solutions, 37112 Rockfield Chalala, Lusaka, Zambia. You can contact us on WhatsApp 076 492 2544, by phone on 097 965 0844, or by email at servicelinkzm@gmail.com.

2. Information we keep

  • Your account: your name, phone number, email address and password (the password is stored in scrambled form and we cannot read it).
  • Business listings: business name, category, area, contact numbers, email, description, social page, services, prices, working hours, logo and photos.
  • Bookings: the service, date and time, your name, phone number, any notes you add, and the booking's status.
  • Reviews: your star rating and comment. Only your first name and the first letter of your surname are shown publicly.
  • Payments for plans: the plan, amount, mobile money network, the number paid from and the transaction ID. We never ask for or store your mobile money PIN.

3. Why we use it

We use this information to run your account, show business listings, send booking requests to businesses, let businesses accept or decline them, publish reviews, switch on paid plans, prevent misuse, and contact you about your account or bookings.

4. Who can see it

  • Anyone can see approved business listings and published reviews.
  • When you book, the business sees your name, phone number, the booking details and your notes, so it can serve you.
  • ServiceLink administrators can see account, booking and payment records to run and protect the platform.
  • We do not sell your information. We share it only where the law requires us to.

5. Where it is stored

ServiceLink's information is held by our database provider, Supabase, on secure servers outside Zambia. Access is protected by passwords and security rules that limit each person to the information they are allowed to see.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account and listing information while your account is open. Booking, review and payment records are kept for up to five years for records and to resolve disputes, then deleted. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

7. Your rights

Under Zambia's Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 you can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, object to how we use it, or ask us to delete it. Send your request to servicelinkzm@gmail.com or on WhatsApp 076 492 2544 and we will reply within 30 days.

8. Terms of use

  • ServiceLink connects you with businesses. The service or product is agreed between you and the business. ServiceLink is not a party to that agreement and is not responsible for the work done, its price or its quality.
  • Payment for services is made directly to the business. ServiceLink does not collect payment for services booked on the platform.
  • Bookings are requests until the business accepts them. Requests that are not answered within 48 hours expire.
  • Businesses must give true information, hold any licences their work needs, and keep their prices and hours up to date. ServiceLink checks listings before approving them and may suspend or remove a listing at any time.
  • Reviews can only be left after a completed booking and must be honest. ServiceLink may hide reviews that are abusive, false or unrelated to the service.
  • Featured placement and Premium plans are paid by mobile money at the price shown before you pay. A plan starts when ServiceLink confirms the payment and runs for the number of days shown. Payments are not refundable once a plan has started, except where the law requires.
  • Do not use ServiceLink to send spam, mislead people or break the law. We may close accounts that do.

Questions about this page? Contact servicelinkzm@gmail.com.
